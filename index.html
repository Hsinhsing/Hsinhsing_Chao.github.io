Research Scientist · AI for Cybersecurity

Hi! I am Xinxing Zhao, a researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. My research focuses on intelligent network intrusion detection, generative models, anomaly detection, and Transformer-based learning.

A central theme of my research is developing learning systems capable of identifying complex and previously unseen cyber threats through representations of normal and malicious network behavior.
